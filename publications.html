Educational Integration of Refugees and Newly Arrived Immigrants · Springer

Bridging or Widening the Gap? High School Resources and Achievement Gaps Across Migration Backgrounds in Italy

Elisa Sbalchiero, Emanuele Fedeli, and Moris Triventi

The chapter examines whether high-school resources help reduce achievement gaps among students with different migration backgrounds in Italy. It shows that access to better-resourced schools does not necessarily equalise outcomes and may, in some cases, widen disparities.
