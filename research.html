Research

How educational inequalities develop over time

My research draws on scholarship in social stratification and mobility and in the sociology of education to examine inequalities in educational opportunities and academic achievement by social and migration background. I also investigate how school contexts and education policies shape these inequalities.

Broad research themes

Research streams

01

Inequalities by social and migration background across educational trajectories

I examine inequalities in education by social and migration background across different stages of the educational trajectory. I am particularly interested in whether schools compensate for unequal starting points or reproduce and widen disparities.

02

School learning contexts and resources

I examine how classroom composition, school climate, and material and human resources shape learning opportunities and achievement gaps.

03

Education policy, reforms, and school interventions

I study the diffusion and implementation of education policies, reforms, and school interventions, and their consequences for student outcomes.

04

Quantitative methods

I use longitudinal, multilevel, and comparative approaches to connect individual trajectories with school- and system-level conditions.
