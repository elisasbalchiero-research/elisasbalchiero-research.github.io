A photographic notebook

Places that shaped me

A visual path through the landscapes I come from, the cities that became home, academic journeys, Paris, and the passions that accompany my research.

All photographs were taken by Elisa Sbalchiero.

01 · Origins

Veneto, mountains, and home

I come from Veneto. The landscape leads toward the mountains, photography, and family; Paganella, above Trento, remains my favourite mountain, and Trento my first home away from home.

02 · Academic path

Cities that became home

The PhD took me between Turin and Milan. Pero was home during the first two years of the doctorate—my second, and perhaps deepest, home.

03 · Research journeys

Conferences, in no particular order

National and international conferences have meant moving through Bamberg, Florence, Modena, Seville, and many places still to come.

04 · Now

Paris, for now

Gardens, flea-market discoveries, rooftops, and familiar monuments: fragments of the city that is home during my visiting period at Sciences Po.

05 · Beyond research

Photography, politics, movements, radio

My strongest passions meet here: photography, politics, social movements, and radio—Radio Popolare in Milan, precisely.
