Unequal learning trajectories: Educational pathways, schools, and social inequality

My work examines how inequalities in educational opportunities and academic achievement develop across students’ educational trajectories and within different school and institutional contexts. I focus on learning environments and education policy reforms in Italy and comparatively across Europe.
